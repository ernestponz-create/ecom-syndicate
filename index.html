What exactly do you do?

We are a done-for-you managed ecommerce service. We build your store from scratch and scale it. One centralized, experienced team handles store build, product sourcing, advertising, international logistics and chargeback protection.

Do I need ecommerce experience?

No. Our team handles the work from A to Z. Many people who contact us are starting from zero, and others already run a store and want help scaling it.

Who pays for advertising?

You pay the ad platforms directly for your ad spend, so you always control that budget. Our ads team manages the campaigns.
