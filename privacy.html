Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The Ecom Syndicate ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or submit a request through our website or our ads on Facebook and Instagram, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, and your answers to our short questions about your situation, readiness and timeline.
  • Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages viewed and referring source, through cookies or similar technologies, including advertising pixels from platforms such as Meta.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request and schedule a free call.
  • To understand your goals so we can prepare for the call.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and measure our advertising.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Calls, texts and email

By submitting a request, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP to a text or using the unsubscribe link in an email.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (for example form handling, scheduling, email and communications, and analytics and advertising platforms), and where required by law. Those providers may use your information only to provide services to us.

Facebook and Instagram lead ads

If you submit your information through a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta shares the details you provide with us, and we use them as described in this policy. Meta's handling of your data is covered by Meta's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and advertising

We may use cookies and advertising pixels to measure performance and show relevant ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing us at the address below. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, which we will honor as required.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the most recent version.
